Intelli-Eye: An Holistic Deep-Learning Architecture for Predictive Threat Analysis and Intelligent Alert Automation

2026 International Conference on Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision (ICNPCV)
IEEE Bangalore Section • Bengaluru, India • IEEE Catalog / CFP26CV9-ART • ISBN: 979-8-3315-9284-4 • ©2026 IEEE

Abstract: The imperative for more advanced automated public safety has brought the deficiencies of prevailing AI surveillance paradigms to the forefront—high computational overhead, a lack of robustness in adverse conditions, and an inability to holistically detect multiple threat categories. The proposed "Intelli-Eye" system is a novel, unified solution specially engineered to detect multiple threats simultaneously, including shoplifting, weapons, fire, and accidents. The methodology utilizes a standardized video preprocessing pipeline coupled with specialized YOLOv8 CNN models for overt threats and a YOLOv8-Pose-powered heuristic engine for complex human behavioral interpretation (Euclidean keypoint velocity and cumulative risk score with time decay). Empirical evaluations on Roboflow and Kaggle datasets confirm a mean average precision (mAP@0.5) of 91.3%, with real-time alerting via Telegram/SMS and automated post-event incident reporting.
