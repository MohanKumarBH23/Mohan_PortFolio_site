Intelli-Eye: An Holistic Deep-Learning Architecture for Predictive Threat Analysis and Intelligent Alert Automation

2026 International Conference on Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision (ICNPCV)
IEEE Bangalore Section • IEEE Catalog / CFP26CV9-ART • ISBN: 979-8-3315-9284-4 • ©2026 IEEE

Abstract: Prevailing automated public safety systems suffer from high computational overhead and an inability to holistically detect disparate threat classes. The proposed Intelli-Eye architecture introduces a unified pipeline coupling fine-tuned YOLOv8 CNN models for overt threats with a YOLOv8-Pose skeletal heuristic engine for human behavioral dynamics (Euclidean keypoint velocity and cumulative risk scoring with exponential decay). Empirical evaluations confirm a mean average precision (mAP@0.5) of 91.3% with real-time Telegram/SMS dispatch and automated incident archiving.
